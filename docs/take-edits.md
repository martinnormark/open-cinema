# Take edits

Four edits that change part of a take and keep the rest: **Make it longer**, **Redo a moment**, **Fix an area** and **Reframe**. Each was tested on real takes on 2026-10-18 before it joined the front page ([task-map.md](task-map.md)). Side-by-sides from these runs are in the project's shared `take-goals/` folder.

| Goal           | Workflow kind   | Runs on                                                                                        | Code                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Make it longer | `video.extend`  | Seedance 2.5 extend on Router, then an append stage on Comfy Cloud; or one LTX 2.3 Cloud graph | [take-edits.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/take-edits.ts), [ltx-fill.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/ltx-fill.ts) (`extend`) |
| Redo a moment  | `video.moment`  | LTX 2.3 on Comfy Cloud                                                                         | [ltx-fill.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/ltx-fill.ts) (`moment`)                                                                 |
| Fix an area    | `video.area`    | LTX 2.3 In-Outpainting IC-LoRA (clear), or Wan 2.1 VACE (words), on Comfy Cloud                | [ltx-fill.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/ltx-fill.ts) (`area`), [wan-vace.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/wan-vace.ts)       |
| Reframe        | `video.reframe` | LTX 2.3 outpaint IC-LoRA on Comfy Cloud                                                        | [ltx-fill.ts](../packages/comfy/src/workflows/ltx-fill.ts) (`reframe`)                                                                |

Every Cloud graph is exported to `workflows/cloud/take-edits/` and validated against Cloud's node catalogue (`bun scripts/validate-workflows.ts`). `bun scripts/ltx-graph.ts` runs any of them against Cloud through the dev relay (`area`, `area-add`, `moment`, `extend`, `reframe`).

## Make it longer

**Seedance 2.5 extend** (`byteplus/dreamina-seedance-2-5-260628`, `omni_reference_task_type: "extend"`, the take as `reference_video`, ratio `adaptive`, 720p). The prompt says it continues `[Video 1]` from its last frame, then the creator's words, then "one continuous shot: no cut, no fade". Only the new seconds come back; the `append` media stage scales them onto the take's size and frame rate and plays them after it, so the take itself is untouched and keeps its sound. A mentioned Character sends its lead reference as `[Image 1]`.

- Street-dance take, 7.6 s plus 4 s: continued from the last frame with no visible cut (`make-longer-street-seedance.mp4`, `make-longer-seam.png`). A first try on the untrimmed take restarted from black, because the take ended on a whip; ending a take mid-action matters.
- Kitchen-table take: refused twice with `content_policy_violation`, most likely for its close real faces. Refusals are not billed, and the error now names LTX as the way round.
- Billing: 237,600 and 259,200 video tokens for the two successful runs, about 1,070 and 1,170 credits. Seedance bills the take's seconds as well as the new ones, about 94 credits a second in all (`seedanceExtendEstimate`).

**LTX 2.3 fallback**: the take's last 17 frames become the first keyframe of a 97-frame generation; the first 17 generated frames are dropped and the rest are joined to the take. On the kitchen take it carried the action on plausibly in 39 s of GPU, softer than the take and about three seconds long. It is the choice for takes Seedance refuses.

## Redo a moment

A window of up to 97 frames around the chosen moment (at most 3 s, with 8 frames of context each side) is generated by LTX 2.3 with four single-frame keyframes from the take: two just before the moment and two just after. The regenerated frames are dissolved in over three frames at each end and spliced back into the whole take with `ReplaceImagesInBatch`, so the result is the full take with its sound.

- Voice take, 2.0 to 3.4 s, "he looks up and smiles, then nods": he did, and the take rejoined smoothly. Mean frame-to-frame difference at the joins went from 1.7 (a visible pop) to 0.7 and 1.0 after the dissolve, against 0.5 inside the take. Identity mostly held. 14 to 31 s of GPU.
- Lightricks' In-Outpainting IC-LoRA with the moment painted out ignored the prompt (no smile), so it was dropped for plain keyframes.

## Fix an area

The creator paints over the first frame; the mask is a black-and-white PNG of at most 512 px, sent inline. The same area is used for every frame.

- **No words: LTX 2.3 with the In-Outpainting IC-LoRA** (`LTXVInpaintPreprocess`, green fill). It removed a glass from the café table cleanly in 19 to 38 s, but ignored requests to add something (no tulips).
- **Words: Wan 2.1 VACE** following Comfy's `video_wan_vace_inpainting` template with the brushed mask held over all 81 frames and a grey fill. It drew a small vase of white tulips that holds for the whole clip, in about 4.5 minutes of GPU at 832 × 480. A first try with a single-frame mask returned a grey block.
- Both put the generated pixels back only inside a soft-edged mask; everything else is the take's own. Both use the first 81 frames (about 3 s).
- **Not shipped**: a Thing as VACE's reference image. With a lamp cut from another take as the reference, the area came back cleared with no lamp, twice (one at full size, one through the production graph). Things go through Swap an object for now; a cut-out reference on white and a larger area are the next test.

## Reframe

Comfy's `template_ltx2_3_lora_video_outpainting`: the take is centred whole on a black canvas of the new aspect (9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9), passed through a gamma of 2, generated on the LTX 2.3 dev checkpoint with the distilled LoRA at 0.5 and Lightricks' outpaint IC-LoRA, then brought back with a gamma of 0.5. The take's own pixels are composited back over the middle with a feathered edge.

- Kitchen take, 16:9 to 9:16: a plausible ceiling, pendant lamp, chair backs and table legs, with no visible seam. 48 s of GPU for 73 frames.
- Cropping halfway to the new shape first (half crop, half new picture) cut the two people off, so the take is always kept whole.
